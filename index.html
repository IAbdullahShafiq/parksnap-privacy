ParkSnap Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ParkSnap handles information when you use the mobile application. ParkSnap helps users save outdoor parking locations and mall parking details so they can find their parked vehicle later.

Information We Collect

ParkSnap may collect or use the following information only to provide app features:

  • Location information, such as GPS coordinates, when you save an outdoor parking spot or attach a location to a mall parking entry.
  • Parking details that you enter, such as mall name, floor, zone, bay, notes, and vehicle details.
  • Photos that you choose to add, such as vehicle images or parking spot images.
  • Purchase status for premium features through Google Play Billing.
  • Advertising data handled by Google AdMob to show ads and rewarded ads.

How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Save your parking location and parking notes.
  • Open your saved parking location in Google Maps.
  • Show saved vehicles and parking photos inside the app.
  • Unlock premium features and remove ads after a valid purchase.
  • Provide rewarded ads that can give in-app coins for unlocking themes.

Local Storage

ParkSnap stores parking records, vehicle details, theme choices, coins, and related app data locally on your device, including local SQLite storage. Photos selected by you may also be stored locally for app use.

Third-Party Services

ParkSnap uses third-party services that may collect information according to their own policies:

  • Google Maps or map apps, when you open a saved parking location.
  • Google AdMob, for ads and rewarded ads.
  • Google Play Billing, for one-time premium purchases.

You can learn more about Google privacy practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not share your saved parking entries, vehicle details, or selected photos with other people. Third-party services such as Google AdMob, Google Maps, and Google Play Billing may process data needed for their services.

Permissions

ParkSnap may request these permissions:

  • Location permission to save your current parking location.
  • Camera or photo permission when you choose to add images.
  • Internet access for ads, purchases, and map-related services.

Children's Privacy

ParkSnap is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Data Control

You can delete saved parking records and vehicle details inside the app. You may also clear app data or uninstall the app from your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date.
